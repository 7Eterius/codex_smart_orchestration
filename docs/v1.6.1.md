# v1.6.1: launch-benchmark tuning

## Source

This patch uses OpenAI's official GPT-6 Sol/Luna launch article published on
2026-09-22:
https://openai.com/index/introducing-gpt-6-sol-and-luna/

The article adds direct benchmark evidence that was missing from the evidence set
used for v1.6.0. It also documents a GPT-6 prompt-caching behavior that changes one
of Smart's older efficiency assumptions.

## Routing changes

| Role | v1.6.0 | v1.6.1 | Evidence/rationale |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| simple_executor | Luna Low | Luna Low | unchanged; bounded established-pattern edits |
| routine_executor | Luna Medium | Luna High | AutomationBench explicitly reports Luna High +5.4 points vs predecessor at 58% lower cost/task |
| default_executor | Luna xhigh | Luna Max | DeepSWE Luna Max 66.6%; OSWorld Luna Max exceeds GPT-5.6 Sol Medium at one tenth its cost |
| senior_executor | Sol Medium | Sol xhigh | AutomationBench Sol xhigh 33.2% vs Opus 5 Max 26.9% at 9% of its cost; OSWorld 60.5% vs 60.3% Opus 5 Medium |
| tester | Luna xhigh | Luna xhigh | unchanged; high-volume independent verification with parent/Senior escalation for material ambiguity |
| companion | Luna Medium | Luna Medium | unchanged; compact context consolidation |
| investigator | Luna xhigh | Luna xhigh | unchanged; bounded evidence discovery |
| archivist | Luna Medium | Luna Medium | unchanged; concise verified project memory |

DeepSWE also reports GPT-6 Sol Max at 68.8% and GPT-6 Luna Max at 66.6%. Agents'
Last Exam reports GPT-6 Sol Max at 56.4%. Those results establish that Max is useful
for hard coding/long-horizon work, but they do not justify making every high-volume
verification or every parent turn Max.

The Senior role is deliberately xhigh rather than Max. It is an escalation worker
for named hard decisions or transferred implementation, while the owner-selected
main still owns final judgment and can use a stronger model/effort when appropriate.

## Cache-policy correction

OpenAI states that GPT-6 can change reasoning effort and tool availability without
breaking reuse of earlier cached context. Smart therefore removes the old rule that
kept tools/MCP and effort stable *for cache continuity*.

New policy:
- Keep owner-selected parent model, working directory, sandbox and approvals stable
  unless the task or owner requires a change.
- Do not keep unnecessary tools exposed or a mismatched effort merely to preserve
  prompt cache.
- Change reasoning effort/tool availability only when the runtime supports it and
  the current task phase benefits.
- Never weaken permissions for caching or efficiency.

This is a cache optimization rule, not authority to silently override an owner's
explicit parent effort. Named worker roles still carry fixed reviewed efforts.

## Other launch evidence

The article says GPT-6 Sol makes about half as many mistakes as GPT-5.6 Sol on an
internal error-inducing factuality evaluation, and higher-effort GPT-6 Luna matches
GPT-5.6 Sol factuality at about one hundredth its cost. It also reports improved
technical collaboration style and lower rates of misleading claims about coding
work. These support Smart's existing use of capable Luna workers with explicit
evidence and independent acceptance; they do not remove verification gates.

OpenAI reports improved prompt-cache hit rates by default and says GitHub observed
more than a 50% reduction in prompt tokens requiring fresh processing across billions
of requests over several months. That aggregate observation is not a forecast for
this workflow or an individual subscription.

## Preserved boundaries

No parent model/effort is automatically rewritten. Tester, design ownership,
independent acceptance, permanent memory, opt-in token reporting, child recursion
guards, project safety, and application-specific gates remain unchanged. Astra is
not added as an automatic worker tier.

## Validation contract

v1.6.1 must pass every prior suite plus dedicated checks for the new three-role
effort map, cache-policy wording, current version, exact v1.6.0 upgrade/reapply/
rollback and project/parent preservation. Static tests verify configuration and
migration, not live benchmark reproduction or weekly-plan savings.
