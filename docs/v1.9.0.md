# v1.9.0: operator/judge cost routing

v1.9.0 fine-tunes v1.8 around a common development workload: many Browser and
Computer Use turns are mechanically expensive in token volume but do not require
expensive judgment.

## Model map

- Simple: GPT-6 Luna Low
- Routine: GPT-6 Luna High
- Default: GPT-6 Luna xhigh
- Senior: GPT-6 Sol xhigh
- Tester: GPT-6 Luna High
- Companion: GPT-6 Luna Medium
- Investigator: GPT-6 Luna xhigh
- Archivist: GPT-6 Luna Medium

Max is no longer used by an automatic named role. It remains available to an owner
who has representative evaluations showing a material gain over xhigh.

## Operator versus judge

Simple is broadened into the low-cost operator lane. Besides small established edits,
it can read webpages, navigate known browser/desktop flows, change explicit development
settings through a GUI, inspect DOM/console/network state, capture screenshots and
check explicit observable acceptance criteria.

The boundary is judgment. Simple must not decide whether a design is good, invent an
interaction model, redefine hierarchy or perform ambiguous visual critique. Those
decisions remain with the main/Senior Sol tier. Astra remains owner-selected for the
rare case where difficult spatial or visual judgment itself benefits from the flagship.

This deliberately separates the model that clicks from the model that judges.

## Effort changes

Default moves from Luna Max to Luna xhigh. Tester moves from Luna xhigh to Luna High.
Routine remains Luna High because normal software implementation still benefits from
strong coding reasoning, especially given early independent reports that Luna's
agentic-coding gains are weaker than Sol's.

The Sol parent baseline remains Medium. When supported safely by the runtime, product,
UX, visual-design and hard architecture decisions may temporarily raise the same
session to High/xhigh and then return to Medium without rewriting the stable prefix.

## Verification economy

A Browser, screenshot or Computer Use task does not automatically justify an
independent Tester. Deterministic GUI evidence can be collected by Simple. Tester is
reserved for independent verification where behavior, state, integration,
accessibility, shared contracts or cross-boundary effects make a second judgment
valuable.

Structured evidence is preferred before screenshots when it answers the question:
source/tests, DOM, console and network state first; visual evidence when appearance or
running interaction is actually under test.

## Why this changed

OpenAI currently positions Luna Low for fine-grained edits, well-scoped problem
solving and simple extraction; Luna xhigh for clear-constraint multi-app work; Sol for
work needing judgment; and Astra for the hardest end-to-end work. GPT-6 Luna and Sol
share the same tool surface, including computer use, so a GUI task does not require Sol.

The OrcaRouter comparison reinforces a separate point: model tier and reasoning effort
are independent cost levers. Its API-price and third-party benchmark figures are not
treated as Codex subscription accounting, but they support routing the many mechanical
inner-loop calls to Luna while reserving Sol for the decision that affects the product.
